About Me

Analytical, detail-oriented professional transitioning into data analytics after 3+ years as a remote QA Engineer at Chasescroll. In that role, I tested and audited complex payment, user sign-up, and event workflows, building a rigorous, evidence-based approach to finding what fails and why.

I now apply that same discipline to data. My current work involves cleaning and modeling a multi-table retail dataset and presenting it through decision-ready Power BI dashboards, supported by SQL, Python, and Excel analysis. I'm driven by uncovering hidden efficiencies, keeping pace with emerging technology, and translating technical complexity into clear business value.
